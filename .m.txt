fix(ui): stop the build notice claiming an older server when stamps match

The notice said the running server was from an older build while quoting two
identical stamps, because the verdict was never computed. `record_daemon_build`
was called from both branches of the check and always stored the pair, and the
command read *presence* of that record as `stale: true`. So a matching daemon
earned a notice about a mismatch that did not exist.

The decision now lives in `build_notice`, a pure function, and the cell is only
a sink for its answer. Both tests pin the halves that mattered: equal stamps
earn nothing, different stamps carry both in order.

Reported by a user, and worth recording why I missed it. The previous commit's
message claimed both directions were verified. The log said
`daemon build stamp matches`, which agreed the whole time -- I took that as the
banner being absent and wrote it up as verified without ever looking at the
window. A log line is a fact about the server, not about what the window did
with it; those are different claims, and only a screenshot answers the second.

Verified on the installed app with the stamps equal: the topbar renders the
brand and the navigation and nothing between them.
